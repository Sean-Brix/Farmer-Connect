# INQUIRY & CHAT SUPPORT (USER)

## Create New Inquiry

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: screenshot of create inquiry form]

- Users can submit inquiries or support requests to administrators.
- Accessible by: All Authenticated Users

**Steps / How to Use**
1. Log in to your account
2. Navigate to "Contact" or "Chat Support" from the main menu
3. Click "New Inquiry" or "Start Conversation" button
4. Fill in the inquiry form:
   - **Subject** (required): Brief title describing your issue or question
   - **Category** (optional): Type of inquiry (Technical Support, General Question, Complaint, etc.)
   - **Message** (required): Detailed description of your issue or question
5. Optionally attach files to provide context (screenshots, documents)
6. Review your inquiry for completeness
7. Click "Submit Inquiry" button
8. A confirmation message will appear and your inquiry is created

**Notes**
- Be clear and specific in your description for faster resolution
- Include relevant details (dates, names, error messages, etc.)
- Attach screenshots if you're reporting a technical issue
- You will receive notifications when administrators respond

## View My Inquiries

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: screenshot of my inquiries list]

- View all your submitted inquiries and their current status.
- Accessible by: All Authenticated Users

**Steps / How to Use**
1. Navigate to "My Inquiries" or "Support Tickets" section
2. View your inquiry history organized by status:
   - **Active**: Open or In Progress inquiries
   - **Resolved**: Completed inquiries
   - **All**: Complete inquiry history
3. Each inquiry shows:
   - Subject line
   - Status (Open, In Progress, Resolved)
   - Creation date
   - Last message timestamp
   - Unread message indicator (if admin has replied)
4. Click on any inquiry to open the conversation and view messages

**Notes**
- Active inquiries appear at the top for easy access
- Unread replies are highlighted with notification badges
- You can have multiple active inquiries simultaneously
- Resolved inquiries remain accessible for future reference

## Send Messages in Inquiry

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: screenshot of inquiry conversation]

- Continue the conversation with administrators by sending additional messages.
- Accessible by: All Authenticated Users

**Steps / How to Use**
1. Open an active inquiry from your inquiries list
2. View the conversation history with all previous messages
3. Type your message in the input field at the bottom
4. Optionally attach files:
   - Click "Attach File" or the paperclip icon
   - Select files from your device
   - Up to 5 files can be attached per message
5. Review your message
6. Click "Send" button to submit
7. Your message appears in the conversation thread
8. Wait for administrator response

**Notes**
- Messages are sent in real-time
- Administrators are notified when you send a new message
- Be patient - response times may vary depending on workload
- You can send follow-up messages if needed for clarification

## View Inquiry Responses

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: screenshot showing admin responses]

- Read responses from administrators to your inquiries.
- Accessible by: All Authenticated Users

**Steps / How to Use**
1. When an administrator responds, you'll receive a notification (if enabled)
2. Navigate to "My Inquiries"
3. Inquiries with unread responses show notification badges
4. Click on the inquiry to open the conversation
5. Scroll to see new messages from administrators
6. Read the response carefully
7. If your issue is resolved, no action is needed
8. If you need further assistance, reply with additional questions

**Notes**
- Unread messages are highlighted for easy identification
- Messages are marked as read once you open the conversation
- Check your email for notifications (if email alerts are enabled)
- Response times may vary based on inquiry complexity and admin availability

## View Active Inquiry

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: screenshot of active inquiry indicator]

- Quickly access your currently active inquiry conversation.
- Accessible by: All Authenticated Users

**Steps / How to Use**
1. If you have an active inquiry, it may be pinned or highlighted
2. Click "View Active Inquiry" or the inquiry notification badge
3. You are taken directly to the conversation thread
4. Continue communicating with administrators as needed

**Notes**
- Only one inquiry may be marked as "active" at a time
- Active inquiry is your most recent or priority conversation
- Easily accessible from dashboard or main menu
- Switch between inquiries if you have multiple active ones

## Download Inquiry Attachments

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: screenshot of file attachment in conversation]

- Download files that administrators have shared in response to your inquiry.
- Accessible by: All Authenticated Users

**Steps / How to Use**
1. Open the inquiry conversation
2. Locate messages with file attachments (indicated by file icons)
3. Click on the attachment preview or file name
4. The file will download to your device automatically
5. Open the file with appropriate software on your computer

**Notes**
- Attachment types may include images, PDFs, documents
- File names show the original filename
- Ensure you have appropriate software to open downloaded files
- Files are scanned for security before being made available
