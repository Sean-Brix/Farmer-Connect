# SEMINAR ENROLLMENT (USER)

## Browse Available Seminars

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: screenshot of seminar enrollment page]

- Users can view all available seminars and training programs offered by the system.
- Accessible by: All Authenticated Users

**Steps / How to Use**
1. Log in to your account
2. Navigate to "Seminars" or "Training Programs" from the main menu
3. Browse the list of available seminars displayed as cards showing:
   - Seminar title
   - Brief description
   - Seminar photo
   - Date and time
   - Location/venue
   - Available slots (if capacity is set)
4. Click "View Details" on any seminar to see full information
5. Use search bar to find seminars by keyword
6. Filter by upcoming, ongoing, or past seminars

**Notes**
- Only upcoming and ongoing seminars are available for enrollment
- Past seminars are shown for reference only
- Registration deadlines may apply for some seminars

## View Seminar Details

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: screenshot of seminar details modal]

- View comprehensive information about a specific seminar before enrolling.
- Accessible by: All Authenticated Users

**Steps / How to Use**
1. From the Seminars list, click on a seminar card or "View Details" button
2. A detailed view will display showing:
   - Full seminar title
   - Complete description and objectives
   - Date and time (formatted clearly)
   - Venue/location details
   - Capacity and remaining slots
   - Requirements or prerequisites (if any)
3. Review all information to ensure you can attend
4. Click "Enroll" or "Register" button if you wish to participate
5. Click "Close" to return to the seminars list

**Notes**
- "Enroll" button is disabled if the seminar is full or registration is closed
- You cannot enroll if you're already registered
- Some seminars may have prerequisites or target audience restrictions

## Enroll in a Seminar

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: screenshot of enrollment confirmation]

- Register yourself for an upcoming seminar or training.
- Accessible by: All Authenticated Users

**Steps / How to Use**
1. From the Seminar Details page, click "Enroll" or "Register Now" button
2. Review your enrollment information (pre-filled from your profile):
   - Your full name
   - Email address
   - Contact number
3. Confirm that you will attend on the specified date and time
4. Click "Confirm Enrollment" button
5. A success message will appear confirming your registration
6. You may receive a confirmation email (if email notifications are enabled)

**Notes**
- You can only enroll once per seminar
- Enrollment is subject to availability (first-come, first-served)
- Check your email for enrollment confirmation and reminders
- You can view your enrolled seminars in "My Registrations" section

## View My Seminar Registrations

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: screenshot of my registrations page]

- View all seminars you are currently enrolled in or have attended.
- Accessible by: All Authenticated Users

**Steps / How to Use**
1. Navigate to "My Seminars" or "My Registrations" section
2. View your enrollment history with tabs or filters:
   - **Upcoming**: Seminars you're registered for in the future
   - **Attended**: Past seminars you attended
   - **All**: Complete enrollment history
3. Each entry shows:
   - Seminar title
   - Date and time
   - Location
   - Your enrollment status (Registered, Attended, Cancelled)
4. Click on any seminar to view full details again

**Notes**
- Status is updated by administrators (e.g., marked as "Attended" after the event)
- This section helps you track your training history
- You may be able to download certificates for attended seminars (if applicable)

## Cancel Seminar Registration

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: screenshot of cancel registration]

- Cancel your enrollment in a seminar if you can no longer attend.
- Accessible by: All Authenticated Users

**Steps / How to Use**
1. Navigate to "My Registrations" or the specific seminar details
2. Locate the seminar you want to cancel
3. Click "Cancel Registration" button
4. A confirmation dialog will appear asking you to confirm cancellation
5. Click "Yes, Cancel" to proceed, or "No" to keep your registration
6. Upon confirmation, your registration is cancelled and the slot becomes available for others

**Notes**
- Cancellation may have a deadline (e.g., must cancel at least 24 hours before)
- You may be able to re-register if slots are still available
- Administrators are notified of cancellations
- Some seminars may not allow self-cancellation after a certain point
