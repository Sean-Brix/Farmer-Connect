## About Page

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: screenshot of about page]

- Learn about the organization, its mission, and the Farmer Connect system.
- Accessible by: All Users (Public and Authenticated)

**Steps / How to Use**
1. Navigate to "About" or "About Us" from the main menu
2. Read information about:
   - Organization background and history
   - Mission and vision statements
   - Services provided to farmers and communities
   - Goals and objectives of the Farmer Connect system
   - Team members or key personnel (if applicable)
3. View organization photos, awards, or achievements (if displayed)
4. Contact information may be provided at the bottom

**Notes**
- About page helps users understand the purpose of the system
- Information is typically static but may be updated by administrators
- Some sections may include downloadable brochures or reports

## FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions) Page

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: screenshot of FAQ page]

- Find answers to common questions about the system and its services.
- Accessible by: All Users (Public and Authenticated)

**Steps / How to Use**
1. Navigate to "FAQ" or "Help" from the main menu
2. Browse the list of frequently asked questions organized by category:
   - Account & Registration
   - Seminars & Training
   - Distribution Requests
   - Equipment Borrowing (EIC)
   - Surveys & Feedback
   - Technical Support
3. Click on a question to expand and view the answer
4. Use search functionality (if available) to find specific questions
5. If your question isn't listed, use the Contact or Inquiry feature to ask administrators

**Notes**
- FAQ is updated based on common user inquiries
- Questions are organized for easy navigation
- Some answers may include links to relevant pages or resources
- Administrators can add new FAQs as needed

## Contact Information Page

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: screenshot of contact page]

- View contact details for reaching the organization or support team.
- Accessible by: All Users (Public and Authenticated)

**Steps / How to Use**
1. Navigate to "Contact" or "Contact Us" from the main menu
2. View contact information including:
   - **Office Address**: Physical location of the organization
   - **Email Address**: Primary contact email
   - **Phone Number**: Support or main office phone line
   - **Office Hours**: Days and times the office is open
   - **Map**: Interactive map showing office location (if available)
3. Copy contact details for your reference
4. Some pages include a "Send Message" form to submit inquiries directly

**Notes**
- Contact information is maintained by administrators
- For immediate assistance, use the Inquiry/Chat Support feature
- Office hours indicate when staff are available to respond
- Map helps users locate the physical office for in-person visits

## Citizens Charter Page

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: screenshot of citizens charter page]

- View the organization's commitment to service standards and user rights.
- Accessible by: All Users (Public and Authenticated)

**Steps / How to Use**
1. Navigate to "Citizens Charter" from the main menu or footer
2. Read about:
   - **Service Pledge**: Organization's commitment to service quality
   - **Service Standards**: Expected turnaround times for requests and services
   - **Rights and Responsibilities**: What users can expect and what is expected of them
   - **Complaint/Feedback Mechanism**: How to report issues or provide feedback
   - **Service Directory**: List of services offered and how to access them
3. Download the Citizens Charter document (PDF) if available
4. Reference this page when filing complaints or inquiries

**Notes**
- Citizens Charter is a transparency and accountability document
- Service standards help users know what to expect
- Mandated by government regulations for public service organizations
- Updated periodically to reflect service improvements
