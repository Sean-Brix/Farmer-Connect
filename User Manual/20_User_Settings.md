# USER SETTINGS & PREFERENCES

## User Settings Page

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: screenshot of user settings page]

- Users can configure their personal preferences and account settings.
- Accessible by: All Authenticated Users

**Steps / How to Use**
1. Log in to your account
2. Navigate to "Settings" from the main menu or profile dropdown
3. The settings page is organized into sections:
   - Profile Settings
   - Preference Settings
   - Notification Preferences
   - Security Settings
4. Click on any section to view and modify settings
5. Changes are saved automatically or with a "Save" button depending on the section

**Notes**
- Settings are personal and don't affect other users
- Some settings take effect immediately, others may require logout/login
- Default values can be restored if needed

## Language Preference

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: screenshot of language settings]

- Choose your preferred language for the system interface.
- Accessible by: All Authenticated Users

**Steps / How to Use**
1. Navigate to Settings → Preferences
2. Locate the "Language" dropdown menu
3. Select your preferred language from available options:

## Theme Preference

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: screenshot of theme settings]

- Choose between Light mode, Dark mode, or Auto (system preference).
- Accessible by: All Authenticated Users

**Steps / How to Use**
1. Navigate to Settings → Preferences
2. Locate the "Theme" or "Appearance" section
3. Select your preferred theme:
   - **Light Mode**: Bright background, dark text
   - **Dark Mode**: Dark background, light text
   - **Auto/System**: Follows your device's theme setting
4. Click "Save" or the theme applies immediately
5. The interface updates to the selected theme

**Notes**
- Theme preference is saved to your profile
- Auto mode switches between light/dark based on time of day or system settings
- Dark mode reduces eye strain in low-light environments
- Theme setting persists across sessions and devices

## Notification Preferences

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: screenshot of notification preferences]

- Control which notifications you receive via email, SMS, or push notifications.
- Accessible by: All Authenticated Users

**Steps / How to Use**
1. Navigate to Settings → Notifications
2. View notification channels (Email, SMS, Push Notifications)
3. For each notification type, toggle on/off your preference:
   - **Seminar Updates**: Enrollment confirmations, reminders, cancellations
   - **Distribution Alerts**: Request status updates, approval/rejection
   - **EIC Notifications**: Borrow approvals, return reminders
   - **Survey Invitations**: New survey availability
   - **System Notifications**: Important announcements, maintenance alerts
   - **Inquiry Responses**: Administrator replies to your inquiries
4. Enable/disable entire channels (e.g., turn off all email notifications)
5. Click "Save Notification Preferences"
6. Confirmation message appears

**Notes**
- You can opt-out of non-critical notifications
- Critical security alerts cannot be disabled
- Email is the primary notification channel
- SMS notifications may require phone number verification
- Push notifications require browser permission

## Change Password

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: screenshot of change password form]

- Update your account password for security.
- Accessible by: All Authenticated Users

**Steps / How to Use**
1. Navigate to Settings → Security or Settings → Profile
2. Locate the "Change Password" section
3. Fill in the password change form:
   - **Current Password**: Your existing password for verification
   - **New Password**: Your desired new password
   - **Confirm New Password**: Re-enter new password to verify
4. Ensure new password meets security requirements:
   - Minimum length (typically 8 characters)
   - May require uppercase, numbers, special characters
5. Click "Update Password" or "Save Changes" button
6. Confirmation message appears upon success
7. You may be logged out and need to log in again with the new password

**Notes**
- Current password is required for security verification
- New password must be different from the current password
- Both new password fields must match exactly
- Password requirements are displayed on the form
- Change your password regularly for security
- Don't share your password with anyone

## Update Contact Information

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: screenshot of contact info section]

- Keep your contact details (email, phone, address) up to date.
- Accessible by: All Authenticated Users

**Steps / How to Use**
1. Navigate to Settings → Profile
2. Locate the "Contact Information" section
3. Update the following fields as needed:
   - **Email Address**: Primary email (may be restricted)
   - **Contact Number**: Phone number for SMS notifications
   - **Address**: Physical address
4. Click "Save Changes" button
5. Verification may be required for email or phone changes
6. Confirmation message appears

**Notes**
- Email address may be used for login (change carefully)
- Phone number is used for SMS notifications and contact
- Address may be required for distribution pickup or seminar attendance
- Verification codes may be sent to confirm email/phone changes

## Privacy Settings

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: screenshot of privacy settings]

- Control your profile visibility and data sharing preferences.
- Accessible by: All Authenticated Users

**Steps / How to Use**
1. Navigate to Settings → Privacy
2. Configure privacy preferences:
   - **Profile Visibility**: Who can see your profile (Public, Registered Users, Admins Only)
   - **Show Contact Info**: Display email/phone on public profile
   - **Activity Visibility**: Show your activity history to others
   - **Data Sharing**: Opt-in/out of anonymized data for system improvements
3. Click "Save Privacy Settings"

**Notes**
- Privacy settings balance transparency with personal privacy
- Admins always have access to your data for system management
- Data sharing is anonymized and used to improve services
- Changes take effect immediately
