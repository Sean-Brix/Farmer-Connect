# SURVEY PARTICIPATION (USER)

## View Available Surveys

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: screenshot of available surveys page]

- Users can view and participate in surveys created by administrators.
- Accessible by: All Authenticated Users

**Steps / How to Use**
1. Log in to your account
2. Navigate to "Surveys" from the main menu
3. View all available surveys displayed as cards showing:
   - Survey title
   - Brief description
   - Estimated completion time
   - Number of questions
   - Your participation status (Not Started, In Progress, Completed)
4. Browse through available surveys
5. Click on any survey card to view details or start filling it out

**Notes**
- Only active surveys are displayed to users
- Completed surveys are marked to prevent duplicate submissions
- Some surveys may be time-limited or have deadlines

---

## Fill Out Survey Form

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: screenshot of survey form filling page]

- Complete survey forms by answering all questions.
- Accessible by: All Authenticated Users

**Steps / How to Use**
1. From the Surveys list, click "Take Survey" or "Start" on a survey
2. Navigate to the survey form page at `/survey/fill/{surveyId}`
3. Read the survey instructions and description
4. Answer each question:
   - **Multiple Choice**: Select one or more options by clicking radio buttons or checkboxes
   - **Text Response**: Type your answer in the text field
   - **Rating Scale**: Select your rating by clicking stars or numbers
   - **Yes/No**: Click the appropriate button
5. Required questions are marked with an asterisk (*)
6. Progress through the survey (progress bar may show completion percentage)
7. Review your answers before submitting
8. Click "Submit Survey" button at the end

**Notes**
- All required questions must be answered before submission
- Some surveys allow saving progress and returning later
- Be honest and thoughtful in your responses
- Your responses help improve services and programs
- Once submitted, responses typically cannot be edited

## Save Survey Progress (if available)

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: screenshot of save progress button]

- Some surveys allow you to save your progress and return later.
- Accessible by: All Authenticated Users

**Steps / How to Use**
1. While filling out a survey, locate "Save Progress" button (usually at bottom)
2. Click "Save Progress"
3. Your current answers are saved
4. You can safely close the survey
5. Return to "Surveys" page later
6. Click on the survey to continue where you left off
7. Your previously answered questions will be pre-filled

**Notes**
- Not all surveys support saving progress
- Saved progress may expire after a certain time period
- Complete and submit the survey before any deadline
- Multiple saves are allowed until final submission

## View My Survey Submissions

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: screenshot of completed surveys list]

- View surveys you have completed and submitted.
- Accessible by: All Authenticated Users

**Steps / How to Use**
1. Navigate to "Surveys" page
2. Use tabs or filters to view:
   - **Available**: Surveys you haven't started
   - **In Progress**: Surveys you've started but not submitted
   - **Completed**: Surveys you've fully submitted
3. Completed surveys show:
   - Survey title
   - Submission date and time
   - Confirmation message
4. Some surveys may allow viewing your submitted responses

**Notes**
- Completed surveys are marked to prevent duplicate submissions
- Submission confirmation ensures your responses were recorded
- You may receive follow-up communications based on survey results
- Some surveys provide results or feedback after completion
