# Seminar Registration Flow

This flowchart details the seminar discovery and registration process for agricultural training programs.

## Seminar Discovery
- **Browse Interface**: Users can view all available seminars with detailed information
- **Rich Information**: Comprehensive seminar descriptions, speakers, schedules, and requirements
- **Status Indicators**: Clear visibility of registration status and availability

## Registration Process

### Prerequisites
1. **User Authentication**: Must be logged in to register for seminars
2. **Registration Window**: Must be within the registration deadline period
3. **Capacity Check**: Seminar must have available participant slots

### Registration Steps
1. **Seminar Selection**: Choose desired agricultural training program
2. **Eligibility Check**: Verify registration window and capacity availability
3. **Registration Confirmation**: Confirm participation commitment
4. **Status Tracking**: Real-time registration status updates

## Registration Management

### Pre-Registration Checks
- **Login Verification**: Ensure user is authenticated before allowing registration
- **Capacity Management**: Prevent over-registration beyond seminar limits
- **Deadline Enforcement**: Respect registration cutoff dates

### Registration Outcomes
- **Successful Registration**: Confirmed participation with "Registered" status
- **Registration Denied**: Clear feedback for closed registration or full capacity
- **Cancellation Support**: Users can cancel their registration if plans change

## Key Features

- **Capacity Control**: Automatic enrollment limits based on seminar capacity
- **Deadline Enforcement**: Registration automatically closes at specified deadlines
- **Status Management**: Real-time tracking of registration status
- **User Control**: Self-service registration and cancellation capabilities
- **Clear Communication**: Immediate feedback on registration attempts and status changes
