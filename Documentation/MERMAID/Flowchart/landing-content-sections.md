# Landing Page Content Sections

This flowchart documents the main content areas of the landing page including programs, organizational information, and news sections.

## Process Overview

The landing page presents key organizational content through structured sections including program showcases, mission/vision statements, and current news updates.

## Key Components

### Programs Section
- **Program Cards**: Visual representations of available agricultural services
- **Carousel Navigation**: Previous/next controls and pagination dots
- **Interactive Display**: Responsive card layout adapting to screen size
- **Direct Linking**: One-click access to detailed program information

### About Section
- **Mission & Vision**: Core organizational objectives and goals
- **Values Display**: Fundamental principles and commitments
- **Service Descriptions**: Detailed explanations of offered services
- **What We Offer**: Comprehensive overview of organizational capabilities

### News & Updates Section
- **Latest Information**: Current agricultural news and announcements
- **Content Cards**: Structured display of news items with dates
- **External Resources**: Links to government agricultural portals
- **Resource Integration**: Connections to Department of Agriculture and ATI

## Content Management

### Dynamic Elements
- **Program Carousel**: Rotating display of available programs
- **News Updates**: Regularly refreshed content from agricultural sources
- **Resource Links**: Current connections to external agricultural resources
- **Service Information**: Up-to-date descriptions of organizational offerings

### Navigation Patterns
- **Section Linking**: Smooth scrolling between content areas
- **External Navigation**: Proper handling of outbound links
- **Internal Routing**: Seamless transitions to detailed pages
- **Responsive Layout**: Optimal display across all device types

## User Interaction

### Program Exploration
1. **Visual Browsing**: Users scroll through program cards
2. **Navigation Control**: Manual carousel control for specific programs
3. **Detail Access**: Click-through to comprehensive program information
4. **Service Discovery**: Clear pathways to available agricultural services

### Information Consumption
1. **Content Scanning**: Easy reading of organizational information
2. **News Browsing**: Access to latest agricultural updates
3. **Resource Discovery**: Links to additional information sources
4. **Service Understanding**: Clear explanation of available offerings
