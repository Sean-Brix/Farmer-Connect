# Seminar Enrollment Flow

This flowchart details the complete seminar registration and participation process for agricultural training programs.

## Seminar Discovery
- **Browse Interface**: Users can view all available seminars
- **Rich Information**: Detailed seminar descriptions, speakers, and schedules
- **Status Indicators**: Clear visibility of registration status and availability

## Registration Process

### Prerequisites
1. **User Authentication**: Must be logged in to register
2. **Registration Window**: Must be within registration deadline
3. **Capacity Check**: Seminar must have available slots

### Registration Steps
1. **Seminar Selection**: Choose desired training program
2. **Registration Confirmation**: Verify participation commitment
3. **Status Tracking**: Real-time registration status updates

## Participation Management

### Pre-Event
- **Registration Status**: Confirmed participants receive updates
- **Cancellation Option**: Users can cancel if plans change
- **Capacity Management**: System prevents over-registration

### During Event
- **Attendance Tracking**: Admin marks actual attendance
- **Status Updates**: Real-time participation status management

### Post-Event
- **Final Status**: Attended/Not Attended marking
- **Record Keeping**: Permanent participation history

## Key Features

- **Capacity Control**: Automatic enrollment limits based on seminar capacity
- **Deadline Enforcement**: Registration closes at specified deadlines
- **Status Management**: Complete lifecycle tracking from registration to completion
- **User Control**: Self-service registration and cancellation capabilities
- **Admin Oversight**: Administrative control over attendance and final status
